Founder's Story & Mission

Why I Built SJS CONNECT

A student project born out of exam season frustration.

The Vision

I created SJS CONNECT during my own high school exam preparation. When final board examinations and school pre-boards were approaching, finding authentic study materials was an overwhelming headache.

Syllabus-aligned notes, model question papers, and past examination papers for our specific school and the JKBOSE board were either scattered randomly across unorganized WhatsApp groups, locked behind commercial scammy paid apps, or completely unavailable anywhere online.

I realized that hundreds of other students were facing the exact same struggle every exam season. So I built SJS CONNECT to solve this once and for all: a clean, fast, distraction-free academic hub for Classes 9, 10, 11, and 12.

No accounts required. No paywalls. No ads. Just verified notes, school exam papers, and revision guides organized for the way students actually study.

Community & Contributors

SJS CONNECT is powered by the student community. Every resource in our library exists because a student took the time to scan their notes or examination papers to help fellow classmates.

Student Founder & Dev Concept, platform architecture, and mobile design
